                        Learning Objectives



 At the end of Grade 2 (Part 2), the child should be able to



 • Recognise and write the ordinal numbers: 1st to 12th

 • Name the position of a member in a group up to 12th

 • Add two numbers (without carrying)

 • Subtract two numbers (without borrowing)

 • Compare capacity of two containers

 • Estimate the capacity of a container using arbitrary units

 • Measure the capacity of a container using arbitrary units

 • Distinguish between day/night and morning/afternoon

 • Name and arrange the days of the week

 • Name and arrange the months of the year

 • Reading and telling time to the nearest hour

 • Multiply numbers one to ten by two

 • Write the multiplication table of 2

 • Multiply two numbers (within 20)

 • Recognise and name coins and notes up to 100-rupee note

 • Choose correct coins for shopping activities

 • Find the total value of coins/notes (sum not exceeding Rs 100)

 • Decompose coins/notes

 • Divide numbers up to 20 by 2
