                        Learning Objectives


At the end of Grade 1 (Part 2), the child should be able to

•    Recognise, read and write numbers 0 to 10 in numerals and in words

•    Count and write numerals in a group (up to 10)

•    Match numerals to a number of items in a group and vice versa

•    Recognise and use ordinal numbers, and name the position of a

     member in a queue up to 5th

•    Perform addition of two numbers (sum not exceeding 10)

•    Recognise and complete number patterns

•    Perform and state orally simple mental calculations involving addition

•    Solve simple mathematical tasks involving addition of two numbers in

     context (sum not exceeding 10)

•    Use ICT as a tool for learning and consolidating mathematical concepts

•    Recognise and name colours and shapes

•    Draw and name 2-D shapes (e.g., Triangle, Rectangle, Square, Circle)

•    Identify and compare shapes in the environment

•    Sort out objects according to one attribute and explain the sorting rule

     (colour, size and shape)

•    Identify and complete patterns using 2-D shapes

•    Sort and compare objects according to length

•    Sort and compare objects according to mass

•    Recognise and name coins up to Rs 10

•    Decompose coins up to Rs 10

•    Distinguish between time concepts (e.g., morning/ afternoon; day/night)
