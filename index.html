We're revamping the app — every tier is free until 31 October 2026

The site is going through a major revamp, so you may hit the odd rough edge or a page that looks half-finished — sorry for the inconvenience, we're on it. While the work is under way all tiers are free for every account, so students can use the whole app to revise for their 2026 exams. No card, no trial, nothing to cancel. We're doing our best not to lose a single account during the transition — your login and everything you do now should be saved and carry over.

Because we're actively pushing changes, expect the odd bit of server downtime over the coming weeks — if a page won't load, give it a minute and refresh. We're also sorry if some progress recorded in previous months didn't carry across when we moved to the new server — we're doing our best to migrate all historical data over, but a few older records may not make the trip. Reach out if something looks missing and we'll do what we can to recover it.

Kids Mode

Log in with a username & 4-digit PIN — no email, no phone number.

  • ⭐XP, levels & badges — 10 levels from Beginner to Legend, with subject-specific badges
  • 🔥Daily streaks & combos — "3 in a row!" banners, sounds and vibration feedback
  • 📚Chapter practice — instant marking, 3-step hints, worked explanations
  • 📝Exam mode — timed papers (10/25/45 min) with flagging, review and a diagnostic report
  • 💪Weak-area drill — automatically targets the chapters they score lowest in
  • ✏️Scratchpad & number pad — draw your working, type answers with unit keys (cm², kg, h)
  • 🔊Read-aloud — any question can be spoken out loud
  • 🔍Search — find any topic by keyword; typos are fine
  • 📲Install it like an app — keeps working when the connection drops
👨‍👩‍👧

Parent Dashboard

Your account owns the family. Sign in with email or Google.

  • 📊See everything — accuracy per subject and per chapter, questions attempted, streaks, XP
  • 📋Set homework — assign a chapter and difficulty, add a note, choose whether answers are revealed
  • 🔒Parent controls — lock chapters, cap the difficulty level, disable exam mode
  • 📅Study timetable — auto-generate a revision plan weighted to your child's weakest topics, then print it
  • 🔔Daily reminders — pick a time; your child gets a push notification to practise
  • 👥Multiple children — each with their own progress, plus a friendly family leaderboard
  • 📧Weekly email digest — a Sunday summary of how everyone did
  • 🛡️One device at a time — a new login signs the old device out, so accounts aren't shared around
👩‍🏫

Teacher & Tutor Mode

Apply when you sign up — an administrator approves teacher accounts.

  • 💬Homework over WhatsApp — share a code and a PIN; pupils just tap the link
  • 🚫No accounts for pupils — they enter a first name and PIN. Nothing to sign up for, no email collected
  • 🎯Pick the content — choose subject, chapters, how many questions, a timer and a deadline
  • 📈Live results — scores as they come in, class average, and the question most pupils got wrong
  • 🔁Grant a retry — per pupil, when someone deserves another go
  • 🔐Marked on our server — scores are re-graded server-side, so they can't be edited in the browser
  • 🤝Separate from families — tutors never see or touch a child's home account, and parents keep their own controls

Tutor and parent are separate roles. A parent account can apply to become a tutor; it is never granted automatically.
