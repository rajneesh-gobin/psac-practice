Read the passage below and answer the questions that follow.5
"On 12 March 1968, thousands of Mauritians gathered in Port Louis for a historic moment. The British flag, the Union Jack, was lowered for the last time, and the new four-coloured flag of Mauritius rose in its place — red, blue, yellow and green. Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam, the leader of the independence movement and the country's first Prime Minister, had guided his people to freedom through peaceful negotiation rather than violence. He became known as the 'Father of the Nation'. Twenty-four years later, on 12 March 1992, Mauritius became a Republic — choosing its own President as Head of State — while remaining part of the Commonwealth. The date 12 March is celebrated as National Day every year."

Study the outline map description below and answer the questions.6
Mauritius is a small island of about 1,865 km² located in the Indian Ocean, approximately 2,000 km off the east coast of Africa. The island has a roughly oval shape. The terrain rises from the coast towards a central plateau. Major rivers flow outwards from the central highlands towards the coast. The island's coastline has many bays, lagoons and white sandy beaches. It also has a smaller island dependency called Rodrigues, located about 560 km to the northeast.
