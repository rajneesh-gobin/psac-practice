Read the passage below and answer the questions that follow.5
"Enslaved men, women and children were brought by force from Africa and Madagascar to work on the French sugar plantations of Isle de France. They had no freedom, no wages, and were treated as the property of their masters. Many attempted to escape into the mountains and forests — these freedom fighters became known as Marrons. The mountain of Le Morne Brabant, in the southwest of the island, became a refuge for escaping enslaved people and is today recognised as a UNESCO World Heritage Site. When slavery was abolished in 1835 under British rule, formerly enslaved people were finally free, but many were forced to remain as 'apprentices' for four more years without true freedom."
