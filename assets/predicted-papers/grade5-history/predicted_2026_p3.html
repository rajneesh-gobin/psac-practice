Read the passage below and answer the questions that follow.5
"When slavery was abolished in 1835, the British sugar planters in Mauritius needed new workers for their fields. They turned to India. Between 1834 and 1921, approximately 450,000 Indian men, women and children arrived in Mauritius under the indenture system. Workers signed contracts to labour on sugar estates for five years in exchange for a small wage, basic housing and food. Most came from the Indian states of Bihar, Uttar Pradesh and Madras. The Aapravasi Ghat in Port Louis was the immigration depot where these workers first set foot in Mauritius. Today it is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, recognising the importance of this great migration of peoples across the Indian Ocean."
