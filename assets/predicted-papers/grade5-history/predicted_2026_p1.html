Read the passage below and answer the questions that follow.5
"When the Dutch sailors first landed in Mauritius in 1598, they found the island completely uninhabited by humans. Dense ebony forests covered much of the land, and strange flightless birds called dodos walked the ground without any fear of man. The Dutch East India Company (VOC) established the first permanent colony in 1638 under Governor Cornelis Simonsz. Settlers cut down enormous quantities of ebony wood to ship back to Europe. They also introduced deer and pigs to the island. However, the colony faced constant hardships — powerful cyclones destroyed their crops, plagues of rats consumed their food stores, and outbreaks of disease weakened the settlers. By 1710, the remaining Dutch colonists abandoned Mauritius for good."
