Question 1 — Multiple Choice (10 marks, 1 each)

(a) C — vernier caliper (measures diameter/small dimensions accurately)
(b) C — second (s) is the S.I. unit of time
(c) C — diagram showing equal angles of incidence and reflection on opposite sides of the normal, both above the mirror surface
(d) B — Force is NOT a form of energy; it is a push or pull that can cause change in motion
(e) A — 0.25 A   P = IV → I = P/V = 60/240 = 0.25 A
(f) C — Current (defined as Q/t, the rate of flow of charge)
(g) B — Only gravitational potential energy (at the highest point, velocity = 0 so KE = 0)
(h) D — The Moon (it does not produce its own light; it reflects sunlight)
(i) B — R = V/I (Ohm's law)
(j) B — Only kinetic energy (just before hitting the ground, height ≈ 0 so GPE ≈ 0, all energy is KE)

Question 2 — Plane Mirror (7 marks)

(a) Image of girl is 50 cm from the mirror (same distance as object). [1]
(b) Any TWO of: virtual / same size as object / upright (erect) / laterally inverted / image is as far behind the mirror as the object is in front. [2]
(c) The image appears to raise its left hand (lateral inversion). [1]
(d) Image of vase in mirror:
Distance of vase from mirror = 50 (girl to mirror) + 120 (vase behind girl) = 170 cm
Image of vase is 170 cm behind mirror.
Girl is 50 cm in front of mirror.
Distance from girl to image of vase = 50 + 170 = 220 cm. [method [1], answer [1]]
(e) Girl moves 10 cm closer → now 40 cm from mirror → image of girl is 40 cm from mirror. [1]

Question 5 — Energy (10 marks)

(a)(i) Non-renewable: Diesel (✓) and Coal (✓) [1 mark each, total 2]
(b) Matching [4 marks, 1 each]:
Energy stored in food → Chemical energy
Energy of a moving car → Kinetic energy
Energy stored in a compressed spring → Potential energy
Energy that enables us to see → Light energy
(c)(i) GPE = mgh = 2 × 10 × 8 = 160 J [formula [1], answer with unit [1]]
(c)(ii) KE = 160 J (by conservation of energy: GPE at top = KE at bottom, with no energy lost) [1]
(c)(iii) KE = ½mv² → 160 = ½ × 2 × v² → v² = 160 → v = √160 ≈ 12.6 m/s
(Accept 4√10 m/s or 12.6 m/s) [substitution [0.5], answer with unit [0.5] — total [1]]
