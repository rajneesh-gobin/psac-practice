(b)(iii) Accept any one of: Start and stop the stopwatch exactly when the pendulum passes through the lowest point / count oscillations carefully / repeat and average / reduce parallax error. [1]

Question 6 — Speed-Time Graph (10 marks)

(a)(i) Bus at constant speed between B and C: 20 − 8 = 12 s [1]
(a)(ii) Maximum speed = 16 m/s [1]
