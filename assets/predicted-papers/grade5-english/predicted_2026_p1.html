SECTION A — READING COMPREHENSION  (15 marks)

Read the following passage carefully and answer the questions that follow.

The Clever Dolphin

Dolphins are among the most intelligent animals in the ocean. They live in groups called pods, which can have up to thirty members. Dolphins use a special language of clicks and whistles to talk to each other. Scientists have found that each dolphin has its own unique whistle, just like a name.

Dolphins are excellent swimmers. They can leap high out of the water and spin in the air. They use this skill to spot fish from above. A dolphin needs to come up to the surface to breathe air because, like us, it is a mammal, not a fish.

Dolphins are very kind to each other. When one dolphin is sick, the others stay close and help it reach the surface to breathe. Many fishermen in Mauritius have seen dolphins swimming alongside their boats, as if they are playing a game. These amazing creatures remind us that the ocean is full of wonder.
