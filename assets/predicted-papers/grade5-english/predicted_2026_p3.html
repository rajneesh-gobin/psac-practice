SECTION A — READING COMPREHENSION  (15 marks)

Read the following passage carefully and answer the questions that follow.

A Good Neighbour

In the quiet village of Tamarin, everyone looked out for one another. Old Mr Bholah lived alone at the end of the lane. He had a small garden where he grew tomatoes, herbs, and colourful flowers. Every morning, he watered his plants and fed the birds that came to his yard.

One rainy season, Mr Bholah fell ill and could not leave his house for two weeks. His young neighbour, Priya, noticed that the curtains stayed shut every morning. She told her mother, who packed a tray of warm food and sent Priya to knock on his door. Mr Bholah was overjoyed. From that day on, Priya visited him every afternoon after school.

Mr Bholah taught Priya how to grow tomatoes and make a pot of coriander soup. In return, Priya helped him with his shopping list and read the newspaper to him. Their friendship showed everyone in the village that a small act of kindness can change a person's life.
