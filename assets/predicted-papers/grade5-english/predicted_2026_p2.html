SECTION A — READING COMPREHENSION  (15 marks)

Read the following passage carefully and answer the questions that follow.

A Day at the School Library

Every Wednesday afternoon, the pupils of Class Five go to the school library. The library is a quiet and tidy room with long shelves full of books. There are books about animals, space, history, science and many exciting adventure stories.

Mrs Ramsamy, the librarian, welcomes the children with a warm smile. She helps them choose books that match their interests. Last Wednesday, she showed the class a new collection of books about famous scientists. Maya was so excited that she borrowed two books at once.

After reading, the pupils sit in a circle and share what they have read. This is called the Book Talk. Each child speaks for two minutes about their chosen book. The teacher says that reading opens a door to a thousand new worlds. The children love the library because it is a place where they can learn and dream at the same time.
