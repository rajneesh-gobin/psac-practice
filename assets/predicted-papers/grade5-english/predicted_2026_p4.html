SECTION A — READING COMPREHENSION  (15 marks)

Read the following passage carefully and answer the questions that follow.

Keeping Our Beaches Clean

Mauritius is famous for its beautiful beaches and sparkling blue lagoons. Every year, thousands of visitors come to enjoy the white sand and warm sea. However, pollution is becoming a serious threat to these precious places. Plastic bags, bottles, and food wrappers often end up on the beach, harming the animals that live nearby.

Last month, the pupils of Standard Five at Quatre Bornes Government School decided to make a difference. With the help of their teacher, Mr Lutchmanen, they organised a beach clean-up at La Preneuse beach. Thirty-two children arrived early on a Saturday morning, wearing gloves and carrying large black bags. In just two hours, they collected over forty kilograms of rubbish.

The children also made posters to remind visitors not to leave litter on the beach. One pupil, Ananya, said: "The ocean is the home of so many animals. If we do not protect it, we will lose it." Their action inspired many parents and local residents to join future clean-ups. Every small step we take today protects our island for tomorrow.
