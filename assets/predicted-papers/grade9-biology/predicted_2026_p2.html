Q Answer / Marking Points Marks
C1 (a) Award 1 mark for each correct structure listed in the correct order (max 4 marks):
Right ventricle → Pulmonary artery (1) → Lungs / capillaries of the lungs (1) → Pulmonary vein (1) → Left atrium (1).
All four structures must be named and in the correct sequence. Accept minor spelling errors if meaning is clear. 		4
C1 (b) Oxygen and glucose diffuse from the blood in capillaries into the body cells; carbon dioxide and waste products diffuse from cells into the blood (1).
Plasma (fluid) is forced out of the capillaries into the tissue fluid / lymph; this carries nutrients to cells. Excess tissue fluid is drained by the lymphatic system as lymph and returned to the blood (1). 		2
C1 (c) Danger: The coronary arteries supply the heart muscle with oxygen and nutrients. If blocked, the heart muscle is deprived of oxygen and cells begin to die, causing a heart attack (myocardial infarction), which can be fatal (1).
Treatment: Any ONE of — coronary angioplasty (balloon / stent insertion) / coronary artery bypass graft (CABG) / medication to dissolve clot (thrombolytics) / statins to reduce cholesterol / lifestyle changes (accept any medically valid answer) (1). 		2
C2 (a) Any THREE from (1 mark each):
1. Produces genetic variation in offspring, helping the species to adapt to changing environments (1).
2. Variation means disease is less likely to wipe out the entire population (1).
3. New combinations of alleles can produce favourable traits (1).
4. Allows evolution through natural selection to occur over generations (1).
Accept any other biologically correct advantage. 		3
C2 (b) Self-pollination: pollen is transferred from the anther to the stigma of the same flower (or another flower on the same plant).
Cross-pollination: pollen is transferred from the anther of one plant to the stigma of a different plant of the same species. (1 mark for correct difference)
Advantage of cross-pollination: produces greater genetic variation in offspring / reduces inbreeding / produces healthier offspring (1). 		2
C2 (c) Method: Any ONE of — cutting (stem / leaf / root cutting placed in compost to root) / grafting (taking a shoot from one plant and joining it to the rootstock of another) / budding / layering / use of tissue culture.
