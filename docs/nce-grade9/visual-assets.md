# NCE Grade 9 — visual assets

Every diagram, graph, map, circuit, apparatus drawing and geometric figure
created for the Grade 9 banks, with its author and licence.

## Status: 1 asset.

## Rules

1. **Original, not copied.** The sample papers show what visual skill is being
   assessed; the asset is then drawn from scratch at comparable complexity.
   Cropping a figure out of an MES paper is redistribution of a copyrighted
   work and is not done here.
2. **Inline SVG, owned by this repository.** It cannot 404, it works offline,
   its contents are known exactly, and its labels stay crisp when a paper is
   printed. This is already the standing preference in `CLAUDE.md` for the
   PSAC banks.
3. **Alt text must never reveal the answer.** A standing rule of this project.
   "A right-angled triangle with one angle marked x" — not "a triangle where
   x is 55°".
4. **Legible at 360px and on A4.** Grade 9 figures carry more labels than PSAC
   ones (dimension lines, angle arcs, circuit symbols, axis scales), so each is
   checked at both sizes. A figure that only works on a laptop has failed.
5. **A diagram question needs a diagram.** Where a syllabus outcome tests
   reading a graph, map, apparatus or figure, a text-only substitute is not an
   acceptable alternative — it assesses something else.
6. **Rendered and looked at, not existence-checked.** A file that exists, has a
   `viewBox` and passes a validator can still be unreadable. Every asset is
   rendered and inspected before it counts as done.

## Traps already known from the PSAC work

⚠ Anything drawn with `aspect-ratio` must define it in **one** place. The
interactive map keeps its ratios in the JS catalogue rather than in `style.css`
precisely because two copies drift.

⚠ Nothing may rely on a regex lookbehind, and nothing may load from an external
host: hotlinked images break offline and can disappear.

⚠ A generated paper prints in black and white. A figure that distinguishes two
regions **by colour alone** is unreadable on paper — use hatching, labels or
line weight as well.

### Notes on the coordinate grid

- **A function, not a fixed file.** It takes the axis range, so the same code
  serves every grid question instead of one SVG being copied and hand-edited.
- **Black and grey only.** A generated paper prints in monochrome, so nothing
  is distinguished by colour: axes are heavier than the gridlines rather than
  a different colour.
- **Alt text describes the grid, never the answer** — "a squared coordinate
  grid with the x-axis and y-axis drawn and numbered from minus one to eight".
  `scripts/test-grade9-maths-content.js` asserts that no alt text contains its
  own answer.
- **Nothing is fetched.** The only `http` in it is the SVG namespace URI.
  The content test checks `src`/`href`/`url()` rather than searching for the
  string "http", which flagged every valid inline SVG on its first run.

### ⚠ Inside an `<svg>`, italic is `<tspan>` — never `<i>`

`i`, `b`, `em`, `span`, `p`, `font` and about forty other tags are on the HTML
parser's **"exit foreign content"** list. Written inside an `<svg>` — which is
exactly what happens when a question's own HTML is reused as a diagram label —
the parser **closes the svg at that tag** and reparses the rest as sibling HTML.
The figure loses everything after that point and the tail prints loose on the
page.

Nothing throws. The question loads, marks and passes every other test. Ten
figures shipped like this — nine here and the NCE 2025 Q24 rectangle in
`ch09_expressions.js` — and only a rendered page showed it.

- Use `<tspan font-style="italic">` / `<tspan font-weight="bold">`.
- `labelledRectangle()` in `ch09_expressions.js` runs every label through a
  local `svgSafe()` that rewrites both, so its callers cannot reintroduce it.
- `scripts/test-svg-figures.js` asserts it across the whole pack, and was
  verified to catch it by re-planting the defect and restoring the file
  byte-for-byte.

### Other rules these figures established

- **A figure is solved from the question's own numbers wherever it can be.**
  `triangle()` computes its vertices from the two given angles, so the drawing
  cannot contradict the words. A hand-placed triangle was tried first and drew a
  99° corner where the question said 75°.
- **The caller names the region, because the relationship IS the position.**
  `parallelLines()` takes `topInRight` / `topInLeft` / `botInRight` /
  `botInLeft` / `botOutRight`. Three questions naming corresponding, alternate
  and co-interior angles had been drawn from one figure with the same two label
  positions; at most one of them could have been true.
- **A helper throws rather than drawing a plausible lie.** `anglesOnLine`
  requires its angles to sum to 180 (it used to share the half-turn out in their
  *ratio*); `anglesAtPoint` requires 360; `lShape` refuses a degenerate L and
  refuses a notch too small to hold its own label.
- **A dimension label anchored `text-anchor="end"` reads right-to-left from its
  x, and an svg clips at its viewBox.** This is how "10 cm" printed as "0 cm" in
  `g9m-vol-004` and "40 cm" as "0 cm" in `g9m-ext-003`. Leave room, and check
  the rendered page rather than the source.
- **Alt text names the figure, never the answer** — "a triangle with each of its
  three interior angles marked by an arc and labelled", not the value.

### ⚠ Counting `<svg>` strings is not visual QA

Batch 10 reported "19 distinct figures" as a shortage. Ten of the figures it
counted were not drawing at all. A count answers "is there a figure"; only
`scripts/nce-print-preview.js` on a sheet of the distinct figures answers
"does it draw, and does it say what the question says". Render before claiming
a figure works.
