# NCE Grade 9 — ICT (N540): empirical exam blueprint

Derived by reading five real MES papers page by page. **Every page of every PDF
was rendered to PNG at 105 dpi and looked at with an image reader.** Nothing
below is inferred from text extraction alone; `pdftotext -layout` was used only
as a cross-check on wording already read off the rendered page, and where a
stimulus is a raster image that is stated explicitly.

| PDF | pages (`pdfinfo`) | pages visually inspected |
|---|---|---|
| `Information-and-Communications-Technology.pdf` (= **2021**) | 20 | 20 / 20 |
| `NCE-Information-Communications-Tech-2021-2022.pdf` (= **2022**) | 24 | 24 / 24 |
| `NCE-2023-Information-and-Communications-Technology.pdf` | 24 | 24 / 24 |
| `NCE-2024-Information-Communication-Technology (1).pdf` | 28 | 28 / 28 |
| `NCE-2025-Information-Communication-Technology.pdf` | 28 | 28 / 28 |
| **total** | **124** | **124 / 124** |

No page was unreadable and no paper is missing pages: on every cover the
sentence "This document consists of *N* questions printed on *M* pages" was
checked against the rendered page count, and all five reconcile (see §1).
Blank `BLANK PAGE` fillers are included in the counts (2022 p.24; 2023 p.24;
2024 pp.27–28; 2025 pp.26–28). 2021 has none.

⚠ **File naming.** The two files whose names carry no single year are dated from
their own covers, which were read: `Information-and-Communications-Technology.pdf`
is headed **"NATIONAL CERTIFICATE OF EDUCATION / March / April 2021"** and
`NCE-Information-Communications-Tech-2021-2022.pdf` is headed **"2021-2022"**.
They are two different sittings, not two copies of one. They are called 2021 and
2022 throughout this document.

⚠ **The 2021 paper also carries a different subject title** — "INFORMATION
COMMUNICATION AND TECHNOLOGY (N540)" — against "INFORMATION AND COMMUNICATIONS
TECHNOLOGY (N540)" on the other four. Same paper code, so it is the same
syllabus subject.

## 2. Mark distribution across parts

Two things are counted, because the paper allocates marks at two different
grains.

**(a) Allocated parts.** The smallest unit the paper attaches a mark value to.
For Q1 (and Q2/Q3 in some years) the allocation is at whole-question level with
"Each item carries one mark" written underneath; those are counted as one
allocation each and reported separately below.

**(b) Scoring items.** The individual points a marker ticks. Almost every mark
in this paper is a discrete point: 15 MCQ items, 10 True/False rows, 5–10
word-bank gaps and 6 matching rows are all one mark apiece.

### 2.1 Bracketed parts, by mark value

Excludes the whole-question allocations for Q1 (15 every year), Q2 in 2021 (10)
and Q3 (10 every year).

| marks | 2021 | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | **total** | **% of parts** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 9 | 8 | 14 | 9 | 17 | **57** | **35.2 %** |
| 2 | 8 | 14 | 13 | 10 | 9 | **54** | **33.3 %** |
| 3 | 8 | 3 | 5 | 7 | 4 | **27** | **16.7 %** |
| 4 | 1 | 2 | 1 | 1 | 3 | **8** | **4.9 %** |
| 5 | 0 | 2 | 2 | 3 | 2 | **9** | **5.6 %** |
| 6 | 2 | 2 | 1 | 1 | 1 | **7** | **4.3 %** |
| **parts** | **28** | **31** | **36** | **31** | **36** | **162** | 100 % |
| marks in these parts | 65 | 75 | 75 | 75 | 75 | 365 | |
| marks in Q1/Q2/Q3 blocks | 35 | 25 | 25 | 25 | 25 | 135 | |

**68.5 % of all bracketed parts are worth 1 or 2 marks.** Nothing anywhere in
five papers is worth more than **6**, and a 5- or 6-mark part is never a piece
of writing — it is always an objective block (a word bank of 5, a matching grid
of 6, a labelling set of 4–5, or a flowchart with 5–6 numbered statements to
insert).

### 2.2 Response mode, by marks

"Select" = the answer is printed somewhere and the candidate circles, ticks,
matches, orders, or copies it from a supplied list. "Write" = the candidate
produces the answer. Within "Write", the sub-count is marks that need at least
a clause (explain / why / advantage / purpose / drawback).

| | 2021 | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | mean |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Select | 45 | 65 | 58 | 65 | 54 | **57.4** |
| Write (word / value / formula) | 31 | 24 | 36 | 30 | 39 | **32.0** |
| Write (needs a clause) | 15 | 11 | 6 | 5 | 7 | **8.8** |
| Draw free-hand | 9 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | **1.8** |

**More than half the paper is answered by selection.** The single largest
allocation for a written explanation anywhere in five papers is **2 marks**
(e.g. 2025 Q9(a), 2024 Q10(a)). There is no essay, no extended answer and no
part that requires more than about three lines of continuous prose.

⚠ **Free-hand drawing existed in 2021 and has not returned.** 2021 Q7(c)(i)
asked the candidate to *draw* a labelled star topology [3] and 2021 Q9(a) to
*draw* the flowchart for a stated scenario [6] — 9 marks of blank-space
drawing. From 2022 onward the same skill is examined by **inserting numbered
statements into a printed flowchart** (2022 Q11(a) [6], 2024 Q11(c) [5]) or by
**writing text into printed flowchart shapes** (2025 Q11(b) [4]). This matters
for a digital practice pack: from 2022 the paper is fully answerable without a
drawing surface.

### Formats that never appear in any of the five papers

- **Essay or extended writing of any length.** The largest prose allocation is 2 marks.
- **Any part worth more than 6 marks.**
- **Any optional / choice question.** All five covers say "Answer **ALL** questions".
- **Arithmetic.** Calculators are banned on every cover and no part requires
  computation beyond reading a value off a printed table (the closest is
  2022 Q11(b) / 2023 Q11(b), whose dry runs are single additions and comparisons).
- **A hands-on practical component.** The syllabus is full of "create a website",
  "produce a comic strip", "create a database table"; the paper tests all of it on
  paper. There is no practical paper in this set.
- **Audio or video stimulus.**

## 4. Visual and stimulus load

Rule used, stated so it can be re-applied: a **graphical stimulus** is a
photograph, screenshot, icon, diagram, flowchart, pictogram or facsimile of a
rendered document. A plain word bank, a plain data table, a True/False grid, a
matching grid and a blank QBE grid are **not** counted — they are answer
furniture, not pictures.

### 4.1 Whole questions carrying at least one graphical stimulus

| | 2021 | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| questions with a graphic | 6 | 6 | 9 | 9 | 9 | **39** |
| questions in the paper | 9 | 11 | 11 | 11 | 11 | **53** |
| **%** | **66.7 %** | **54.5 %** | **81.8 %** | **81.8 %** | **81.8 %** | **73.6 %** |

The three most recent papers are settled at **9 of 11 questions carrying a
picture**. The two that never do are Q3 (True/False) and one of the
database / internet questions.

### 4.2 MCQ items (Q1) carrying a picture

| | 2021 | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| picture items | 3 | 6 | 13 | 13 | 7 | **42** |
| items | 15 | 15 | 15 | 15 | 15 | **75** |
| **%** | 20 % | 40 % | **86.7 %** | **86.7 %** | 46.7 % | **56.0 %** |

⚠ **This is the most volatile number in the paper.** It swung from 3/15 (2021)
to 13/15 (2023 and 2024) and back to 7/15 (2025). A pack that assumes every
Q1 item needs artwork will over-build; one that assumes none will fail the
2023/2024 shape outright. Build to roughly half.

Kinds of picture used in Q1, all seen: product photographs (mouse, printer,
pendrive, plotter, NIC, router), brand logos (Google, Gmail, Skype, Chrome,
Windows, Android, Linux, AVG, Facebook, Twitter, Wikipedia), Office ribbon
icons, keyboard-key glyphs, flowchart shapes, network-topology line drawings,
speech-balloon outlines, posture line drawings, and one spreadsheet screenshot.

⚠ **Brand logos are used freely and are load-bearing** — 2023 Q1(h), 2023 Q1(l),
2024 Q1(l), 2025 Q1(c) and 2025 Q1(n) cannot be answered without recognising a
specific commercial logo. Reproducing this in a practice pack is a licensing
question, not just an artwork question.

### What the year-to-year swings actually say

⚠ **Four topics have hit ZERO in at least one paper.** Networks fell from 22
(2021) to **1** (2025). Databases scored **0** in 2021 and 8–11 since.
Troubleshooting scored 10 and 6 in 2021/2022 and **0 in both 2024 and 2025**.
Presentation scored 0 in 2021. **One paper is not a blueprint for this
subject** — the same lesson the maths and science packs in this repo already
paid for. Five years is the smallest honest sample.

⚠ **The three stable spines are Word processing, Spreadsheets and Algorithms.**
Each lands between 9 and 20 marks in every one of the five papers, and each
owns a whole Section B question every year. Together they are **38.2 %** of the
five-year total, and they never both spike or both vanish.

⚠ **Networks and Internet services have traded places.** Networks 22 → 17 → 4 →
5 → 1; Internet services 4 → 5 → 7 → 14 → 13. The subject matter did not leave
the paper — it moved from *how a network is wired* (topologies, VPN, NIC,
modem, components) to *what people do online* (e-banking, video conferencing,
social networking, email, search). A pack weighted from 2021 alone would be
almost exactly wrong for 2025.

⚠ **Hardware went from 0 to 23 in five years** and is now the single largest
topic. 2021 has no hardware-device question of any kind; 2025 spends 23 marks
on devices, components, memory units and specifications.

## 6. Numbering depth and answer-line conventions

**Depth actually used: three levels, never four.**

```
Question 7 (7 marks)
  (a)                      ← level 2
  (b)  (i)                 ← level 3   ← deepest anywhere in five papers
       (ii)
```

| | level 1 | level 2 | level 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2021 | Question 1 … 9 | (a) (b) (c) … | (i) (ii) (iii) (iv) |
| 2022 | Question 1 … 11 | (a) (b) …, and **(A) (B)** in Q2 | (i) (ii) (iii) |
| 2023 | Question 1 … 11 | (a) (b) … | (i) … (v) |
| 2024 | Question 1 … 11 | (a) (b) … | (i) (ii) (iii) |
| 2025 | Question 1 … 11 | (a) (b) … | (i) (ii) (iii) |

⚠ **Q1's item labels are not stable.** 2021, 2023, 2024 and 2025 label the 15
MCQ items **(a)–(o)**; **2022 labels them (i)–(xv)**. 2022 Q2 is also the only
place a paper uses capital **(A) / (B)** as a sub-level. Q3's True/False rows
are `(a)`–`(k)` in 2021/2023/2024/2025 and bare `a)`–`k)` in 2022.

⚠ **Deepest allocation is at level 3.** 2021 Q6(a)(iv) [2], 2022 Q10(b)(iii)
[2], 2023 Q11(c)(ii) [3], 2024 Q9(b)(iii) [3], 2025 Q10(b)(ii) [4].

### Answer-line conventions, measured

- **Marks are printed right-aligned in square brackets, `[n]`, at the end of the
  part** — stated on every cover as instruction 8. The question header
  additionally repeats the total, e.g. `Question 8 (10 marks)`.
- A **narrow ruled "Marks" column runs down the right edge of every question
  page**, with small `E` / `TL` / `QC` / `CE` boxes at the foot of the last page
  of each question for examiner initials. (2021/2022 covers carry a Quality
  Controller row; 2023–2025 do not.)
- **Written answers sit on dotted leader lines** (`…………………`), one line for a
  1-mark answer, two or three for 2–3 marks. The line count is a reliable signal
  of the expected length and is worth reproducing.
- **"Give two…" answers are pre-numbered** `1. …………` / `2. …………`, one leader
  line each; "give three" gets `1.` `2.` `3.`.
- **Tick answers use a drawn square box** ▢ beside each alternative, or a
  dedicated `Tick (✓)` column in a table.
- **Matching answers go into an "Answer Grid"** — a two-row table with the
  Column A labels `(i)…(vii)` as headers and a blank cell under each, with the
  worked example already filled in bold.
- **Flowchart and QBE answers are written into printed empty shapes / cells.**
- Footers: page number centred, `©MES` bottom-left, `Please turn over` bottom-right
  on the recto pages. The last content page carries `- END OF PAPER -` /
  `END OF PAPER`.
- **A worked example is given wherever the response format is unusual** — the
  True/False table, the matching grid, the classification grid, the ordering
  table and the flowchart-insertion task all show one answer already done. This
  is a consistent authoring convention and should be copied.

## 7. What the papers test that the syllabus does not list

Checked against **§8.0 of the MIE *Nine-Year Continuous Basic Education
Syllabus, Grades 7 to 9*** (`past-papers/syllabus/NCF 7 to 9_230524.pdf`,
PDF pp. 134–151), which was read for this section: §8.5 Scope and Sequence and
the ACL tables §8.7.1 (Grade 7), §8.7.2 (Grade 8) and §8.7.3 (Grade 9). The
syllabus is cumulative and the paper draws on all three grades.

**1. Python is named in the papers and nowhere in the syllabus.** The syllabus
says only "Write computer programs for simple problems" (Grade 9, Level 2) and
names **Scratch / Logo** at Grade 7. The papers test **Python by name** —
reading a printed `if/else` block and predicting its output (2023 Q11(c)(i)),
rewriting a line to use keyboard input (2023 Q11(c)(ii)), completing a `while`
loop with `print()` (2024 Q11(b)), and writing input/output statements from
scratch (2025 Q11(c)). That is **5, 3 and 3 marks** in the last three years, on
a language the syllabus never mentions.

**2. The query-by-example grid is a paper convention, not a syllabus outcome.**
The syllabus says "Create queries, form and reports" and "Create and modify a
database structure". It does not name Microsoft Access, or the QBE grid. The
papers put a **Field / Table / Sort / Show / Criteria / Or grid** in front of
the candidate in **all four** papers from 2022 (2022 Q10(b), 2023 Q10(b),
2024 Q9(b), 2025 Q10(b)) and ask them both to read one and to complete one —
5–7 marks a year. Nothing in the syllabus prepares a candidate for that specific
notation.

**3. Named spreadsheet functions and the conditional-formatting dialog.** The
syllabus says "Use advanced Formatting, Formulae and Functions". The papers name
**IF, COUNTIF, SUM, AVERAGE, MIN** and put a real **conditional-formatting
dialog box** (Less Than / Between / Greater Than) on the page in **all five**
papers (2021 Q8(c), 2022 Q9(e), 2023 Q9(e), 2024 Q8(e), 2025 Q8(f)), asking
which cells the rule will format. `COUNTIF` alone is worth a mark in 2022, 2023
and 2024. None of these names appears in §8.

**4. Reading an operating-system "About / System" panel.** Q6 in 2023, 2024 and
2025 (and its plain-table equivalent in 2022) is 5–7 marks of reading a Windows
system-information screenshot for device name, processor model, processor speed,
installed RAM and OS edition. The syllabus has "unit of measurement of CPU
speed" (Grade 7) and "unit of measurement of storage devices" (Grade 8) but no
outcome about interrogating a system panel. This is **20+ marks over four
papers** on a skill the syllabus does not describe.

**5. Naming Office ribbon icons from a picture.** 2023 Q2(a) + Q5(a) = 8 marks;
2024 Q2(a) + Q5(a) = 8 marks; 2025 Q2(c) = 3 marks. The syllabus asks learners
to *perform* formatting; the paper asks them to *recognise the toolbar glyph*
for Cut, Save, Print, WordArt, Copy, Subscript, AutoSum, SmartArt, Font Colour,
Strikethrough, Filter, Sort, Format Painter, Notes Page, Justify, Bullets and
Numbering.

**6. Sensors.** 2023 Q4(b) (temperature sensor), 2024 Q11(c) (a greenhouse
cooler flowchart driven by a temperature sensor, 5 marks) and 2025 Q4(a)
(sensor as an input device). The Grade 7–9 ICT syllabus does not name sensors;
the nearest outcomes are "Model using ICT" (Grade 8) and "Perform mathematical
calculations and modelling".

### The reverse — syllabus outcomes the recent papers barely test

- **Website creation.** Grade 8 "Create a website" and Grade 9 Level 3 "Create a
  user-friendly website that also includes multimedia elements". Tested **once
  in five years** — 2021 Q7(d), 3 marks of HTML-to-render. Absent 2022–2025.
- **Comic strips.** A full Level 1/2/3 strand at Grade 9 ("Produce a comic strip
  to fit a purpose"). It survives in the papers only as **1–2 marks a year of
  speech-balloon recognition** (2022 Q1(xi), 2023 Q1(j), 2024 Q1(h), 2025 Q1(b),
  2025 Q3(g)) and never as a production task.
- **Presentation.** The syllabus gives slide masters, design templates, notes
  and handouts, and Print Preview a whole strand. The papers give the topic
  **3.4 marks a year on average**, always as MCQ or True/False recall.
- **Troubleshooting** is an explicit Grade 9 Level 2 outcome ("List the steps in
  troubleshooting a particular computer system problem") and it scored
  **0 in 2024 and 0 in 2025**. It is the clearest case in this subject of a
  named outcome the current papers ignore — and, being explicitly on the
  syllabus, the clearest candidate to come back.

### 8.4 Caveats on this proposal

- ⚠ **The weights are a five-year mean of a moving target.** Networks would be
  weight **9** on 2021 alone and weight **0** on 2025 alone. Troubleshooting
  would be **4** on 2021 and **0** on the last two papers. The mean is the right
  estimator here, but it is an estimator — do not present these numbers as the
  shape of any single future paper.
- ⚠ **Two topics may be mis-split.** "Internet & online communication" versus
  "Networks" is a judgement about where each mark belongs, and the papers
  themselves put both in the same Section B slot in some years. Moving a mark
  between them changes both weights.
- ⚠ **`makeCloze` and free-text formula answers are both awkward for exam
  assembly in this codebase.** `isPoolQuestion()` in `engine/questions_engine.js`
  excludes `cloze` from every pool, and a paper where a third of the marks are
  "write the formula" cannot be assembled from MCQs alone without changing what
  is being tested. This needs a decision before any question is written.
- ⚠ **Brand logos and product photographs carry licensing obligations.** The
  real paper uses them freely; this repo's `assets/questions/provenance.json`
  discipline (a recorded licence for every bundled image) would have to cover
  them, and a Google or Skype logo is not CC-licensed. Prefer generic
  illustrations, and expect that to make some 2023/2024-style items unwritable
  as printed.
- ⚠ **No mark scheme was available.** Everything above is read off the question
  papers. Where a part says "[2]" this document records two marks; it does not
  know how the two marks are split, and for the "explain" parts that split is
  exactly the information a question author would want.
