Derived by reading five real Mauritius Examinations Syndicate NCE French papers
page by page. **Every number below was measured from the PDFs**, not carried
forward from any other document. No passage is reproduced; questions are cited
by number only.

## 1. Per-year structure

Every paper is identical in skeleton. The one substantive change in five years
is that **Question 1 shrank from 15 items to 14** in 2023, and Q3's stimulus
changed genre once.

| | 2021 | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Paper code | N520 | N520 | N520 | N520 | N520 |
| Duration | 2 h 15 min | 2 h 15 min | 2 h 15 min | 2 h 15 min | 2 h 15 min |
| Total marks | 100 | 100 | 100 | 100 | 100 |
| Named sections | **none** | none | none | none | none |
| Questions | 10 | 10 | 10 | 10 | 10 |
| Optional questions | Q10 (A **or** B) | same | same | same | same |
| Mark-bearing parts (taking the 10A route) | **53** | **54** | **53** | **52** | **54** |
| Q1 items | 15 (lettered **A** 1–7 / **B** 8–15) | 15 (1–7 / 8–15) | 14 (1–7 / 8–14) | 14 | 14 |
| Q3 stimulus genre | illustrated **fiche** (gardening advice sheet) | courriel | courriel | courriel | courriel |
| Answer-choice marker on Q9 | fill-in line | fill-in line | tick boxes ☐☐☐ | tick boxes | tick boxes |
| Examiner grid rows | Marker · TL · **QC** · CE/ACE | Examiner · TL · **QC** · CE/ACE | Examiner · TL · CE/ACE | same | same |

There is **no Section A / Section B / Section C** anywhere. The paper is a flat
run of ten numbered questions, and the per-question mark totals are printed in
the heading (`Question 8 (20 points)`).

### The ten questions, and their marks — identical in all five years

| Q | Task | Marks | Stable across 2021–2025? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Grammar: 7 four-option MCQ + 7–8 sentence-transformation instructions | **15** | yes |
| 2 | Vocabulary: 5 four-option MCQ (gap in a sentence) | **5** | yes |
| 3 | Comprehension of an authentic document (courriel / fiche) | **10** | yes |
| 4 | Word formation — write the correct derived form of a given root | **5** | yes |

| 9 | Free composition, ~200 words, choice of 3 subjects | **15** | yes |

## 2. Mark distribution

Counting the smallest unit that carries a bracketed mark (a "part"). Q4, Q5, Q6,
Q7 and Q9 print one bracket for the whole question, so each counts as one part
even though Q4/Q5/Q6 each contain five separately-markable gaps.

### Parts by mark value

| Marks per part | 2021 | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | total | % of all parts |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | 42 | 42 | 42 | 40 | 43 | **209** | **78.3 %** |
| **2** | 4 | 5 | 4 | 5 | 4 | **22** | 8.2 % |
| **3** | 0 | 1 | 0 | 0 | 0 | **1** | 0.4 % |
| **4** | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 1 | **1** | 0.4 % |
| **5** | 5 | 4 | 5 | 5 | 4 | **23** | 8.6 % |
| **10** | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | **5** | 1.9 % |
| **15** | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | **5** | 1.9 % |
| **total parts** | **53** | **54** | **53** | **52** | **54** | **266** | 100 % |

Each column reconciles to exactly 100 marks. Worked example, 2024:
`40×1 + 5×2 + 5×5 + 1×10 + 1×15 = 40 + 10 + 25 + 10 + 15 = 100`.

### What that shape means

- **Nearly four parts in five are worth one mark.** A one-mark French answer here
  is a phrase, not an essay — often three to eight words on a single dotted line.
- **Two-mark parts are almost always "give 2 answers", split `(i)` / `(ii)`**
  with a line each (2024 Q3.8, 2025 Q3.3, 2023 Q3.5, 2021 Q3.2, 2022 Q8.4). The
  exceptions are Q1's gender/pronoun transformations (2024 Q1.11, 2025 Q1.13,
  2023 Q1.11), where two marks buy one sentence with two things to change.
- **The 5-mark parts are of two completely different kinds** and must not be
  taught as one: three of them (Q4, Q5, Q6) are 5 × 1-mark gaps printed under a
  single bracket, and two (the Q8 synonym item, the Q10 final question) are a
  genuine 5-mark block — the synonyms are five 1-mark lookups, the Q10 question
  is one extended paragraph judged as a whole.
- The lone 3-mark part is 2022 Q8.13 (three synonyms instead of five); the lone
  4-mark part is 2025 Q8.16 (four synonyms). Both are the same item type shrunk.

### Marks concentrated in very few parts

The three largest parts — Q9 (15), Q7 (10) and the Q10 closing question (5) —
carry **30 of the 100 marks between them, in three parts out of ~53**. A child
who cannot write connected prose loses 30 % of the paper before any grammar is
tested.

### Formats absent from all five papers

These matter because several are staples of the app's existing PSAC French packs
and would be wasted preparation here:

- **vrai / faux avec justification** — never once.

- **appariement / matching** — never.
- **résumé or compte-rendu** — never, despite the syllabus naming it.
- **description d'image** as a production task — never (2021's Q3 fiche is

- **dialogue à rédiger** — never.
- **QCM with 3 options** — never; every MCQ in five papers has exactly four,

- **transposition discours direct ↔ indirect** — never.

## 5. Gap-fill and cloze — exact mechanics

Three of the ten questions are gap-fill, and they are three *different* skills.
Measured on every paper:

| | Q4 « Écris la forme correcte des mots entre parenthèses » | Q5 « Corrige les erreurs soulignées » | Q6 « Complète le texte avec un mot convenable » |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gaps / items per paper | **5** (2021–2025, all five) | **5** underlined errors | **5** |
| Worked example printed | **yes**, 1, in a handwriting-style face | **yes**, 1 | **yes**, 1 |
| **Word bank given?** | **NO** — the root word is given in bold parentheses | n/a | **NO** |
| **Spare word?** | **no bank, so no spare** | n/a | **no bank, so no spare** |
| Answer written | on a dotted gap in the running line | on a solid ruled line under each text line | on a dotted gap in the running line |
| Constraint stated | derive the right form | correct the underlined word only | « **un seul mot par tiret** » |

**This is the single biggest divergence from the PSAC French papers the app
already models.** PSAC Q6 (« Textes à Trous ») supplies a word bank with a
deliberate spare word; the NCE Q6 supplies nothing at all. A child trained only
on bank-supported cloze has never done the NCE task.

What the Q6 answers actually are, read off all 25 gaps: mostly **grammar
function words** — prepositions (`dans`, `de`, `au`, `sans`), relative pronouns
(`qui`), determiners (`une`, `des`, `chaque`), conjunctions (`quand`,
`lorsque`, `pendant`), negation/degree adverbs (`jamais`, `si`, `très`) —
with a minority of content verbs and nouns (`découvrir`, `réchauffer`,
`attrapé`, `piège`, `peur`). Several gaps legitimately accept more than one
word, which is why the rubric says *un mot convenable*, not *le mot*.

Q4's 25 measured derivations, classified by target part of speech:
**nominalisation 11** (`voyage`, `vitesse`, `motivation`, `impatience`,
`localité`, `douceur`, `neige`, `hiver`, `cuisine`, `pratique`, `âges`) ·
**adjectif 6** (`raisonnable`, `supplémentaire`, `splendide`, `glacé`,
`étonnante`, `favori`) · **adverbe 3** (`rapidement`, `lentement`,
`régulièrement`) · **nom de personne 2** (`journaliste`, `étudiant`) ·
**verbe 2** (`abriter`, `travaille`) · **nom de quantité 1** (`douzaine`).

Q5's 25 measured corrections fall into: **homophones grammaticaux** (`est/et`,
`a/à`, `ces/ses`, `ce/ceux`, `des/de`, `pris/prix`, `pleine/plaine`) ·
**accords** (nom, adjectif, verbe-sujet) · **participe passé vs infinitif**
(`organiser/organisé`, `changé/changer`, `visiter/visité`, `prit/pris`) ·
**orthographe lexicale et accents** (`music`, `vancances`, `Aujourdui`,
`captain`, `seré`) · **déterminants** (`une/un village`, `Cet/Cette année`).
That classification is mine, from reading the underlined words; the paper does
not label them.

## 7. Numbering depth and answer-line conventions

Measured across all 120 pages.

- **Numbering never goes deeper than three levels**:
  `Question 8` → item `13.` → sub-item `(i)`. There is no fourth level.
  2021 alone adds a lettered block level inside Q1 (`A :` items 1–7, `B :`
  items 8–15); 2022–2025 use an unlettered bold instruction line instead.
- Sub-items are `(i) (ii) (iii)…` when they are parallel answers to one
  question, and `(a) (b)` when they are two different questions about the same
  material (2021 Q3.5(a)/(b), 2023 Q8.1(a)/(b)). Q9's three composition choices
  are always `(a) (b) (c)`.
- **Marks are printed in square brackets, right-aligned**, at the end of the
  part. 2021 is the exception on Q1: no per-item brackets are printed at all,
  and the 1-mark-per-item split is inferred from 15 items sharing 15 points —
  2022 states the rule explicitly (« Un point est alloué pour chaque item »).
- Every page carries a right-hand **`Marks`** rule, and pages that close a
  question carry a small stacked box for **E / TL / CE** (plus **QC** in 2021
  and 2022 only).
- **Answer lines come in four shapes, and the shape signals the answer length**:
  1. a single dotted leader line — a one-mark phrase (2024 Q3.1);
  2. two dotted lines — a one-mark answer that needs a clause (2024 Q10A.4);
  3. ~10 dotted lines inside the `Marks` rule — the 5-mark literary question
     (2024 Q10A.6, 2025 Q10B.6);
  4. a **framed box** of dotted lines — the guided writing task (Q7 in all five
     years) and, in 2021 only, Q4 and Q8's stimulus.
- Q5 is unique: the text is printed double-spaced and each printed line is
  followed by a **solid ruled line**, so the correction is written under the word
  it replaces. The worked example is printed on that ruled line in a
  handwriting-style face.
- Q7 prints the salutation and sign-off inside the box in three of five papers
  (2022 `Cher Léo,` / `Amitiés,` · 2024 `Cher Richard,` / `Bisous` · 2025
  `Chers amis,` / `Bisous,`) and leaves the box empty in 2021 and 2023 — so a
  child cannot rely on the frame being supplied.
- Q9's choice is recorded by **tick box** `(a)☐ (b)☐ (c)☐` in 2023–2025 and by a
  **fill-in line** `Rédaction choisie : ……` in 2021–2022.
- The Q10 either/or is stated **twice**: on the cover (instruction 6) and above
  the question.
- Q4, Q5 and Q6 always end their rubric with « **Un exemple est donné.** »
- 2025 alone prints **`END OF PAPER`** after the last question.

## 8. Constants worth writing on the wall

Facts that held in **all five** papers and are therefore safe to build on:

1. Ten questions, 100 marks, 2 h 15, no named sections.
2. The per-question mark map never changes: 15·5·10·5·5·5·10·20·15·10.
3. Every MCQ has exactly four options, A–D, and the instruction is always
   « Encercle la bonne réponse ».
4. Q9 always offers exactly three subjects, always in the order
   **(a) récit · (b) description · (c) opinion**, always ~200 words.
5. Q7 is always 50–75 words with **3 to 5 numbered content points that must all
   be covered** (3 points: 2021, 2023, 2025; 5 points: 2022, 2024).
6. Q10 is always the same two set works — *Le Papa de Simon* (Maupassant) and
   *Topaze* (Pagnol) — and always ends with one 5-mark extended answer that
   invites the child to draw on **the whole work**, not just the printed extract
   (« En te basant sur les extraits étudiés en classe… », 2023 Q10A.6; « En
   t'appuyant sur la nouvelle… », 2025 Q10A.6).
7. Q4, Q5 and Q6 always have exactly five items and one worked example.
8. No word bank is ever supplied for any gap-fill.

## Why the current five chapters do not fit

`subjects/grade9-french/_manifest.js` currently declares five chapters:

| current chapter | current `examWeight` | marks it can earn on a real NCE paper |
|---|---|---|
| `g9fr-co` Compréhension orale | 3 | **0** |
| `g9fr-eo` Expression orale | 3 | **0** |
| `g9fr-ce` Compréhension écrite | 4 | 40 |
| `g9fr-ee` Expression écrite | 4 | 25 |
| `g9fr-litterature` Littérature | 2 | 10 |

Two problems, both measured:

- **6 of the 16 weight points buy questions the written exam does not contain.**
  Oral comprehension and oral expression appear nowhere in five papers.
- **35 of the 100 marks have no chapter at all.** Q1 (15), Q2 (5), Q4 (5),
  Q5 (5) and Q6 (5) are language-manipulation tasks; the manifest's four skills
  plus literature have nowhere to put them. That is the largest single block
  after the narrative comprehension, and it is on every paper.

The one weight that is right is `g9fr-litterature`: 2 of 16 ≈ 12.5 %, against a
measured 10 %.

### Chapters deliberately NOT proposed

- **No image-description chapter**, unlike the PSAC French packs. The NCE never
  asks for one.

## Question-type consequences for the app

- `g9fr-textes-trous` needs the existing **`cloze`** type but **without a word
  bank** — the NCE variant is the typed-gap half only (`gapAlts` is essential:
  many gaps legitimately accept two or three words).
- `g9fr-correction` has no existing type. It is closest to a **`text`** item:
  show one sentence with one underlined error, accept the corrected word. Five
  such items reconstruct one exam question.
- `g9fr-formation-mots` maps onto the existing PSAC "Formation des Mots" pattern
  (`text` items, root word supplied) and can reuse that authoring shape directly.
- `g9fr-transformation` is `text`; each item is one instruction + one sentence.
- `g9fr-ecrit-guide` and `g9fr-redaction` are **not auto-gradable** and should
  follow the past-paper model — read-only prompts with a mark scheme reveal —
  rather than being dealt into a scored exam. Together they are 25 % of the real
  paper, which is worth stating on the screen rather than hiding.

## What the papers test that the syllabus does not list

Measured against the `syllabus:` prose currently in
`subjects/grade9-french/_manifest.js`:

1. **Language manipulation, 35 marks a year, is not in the syllabus at all.**
   The five chapters name four skills plus literature; none of them covers
   sentence transformation, derivational morphology, error correction, open
   cloze or lexical MCQ.
2. **The set works are named and fixed; the syllabus is not.** It says
   « Analyser des textes relevant de différents genres littéraires ». The paper
   says *Le Papa de Simon* and *Topaze*, every year, as a 10-mark either/or.
   A child can revise two named books; they cannot revise "different genres".
3. **The syllabus's oral strands carry no marks.** 6 of 16 current weight points
   are spent on skills the written paper never touches.
4. **Word formation is a named 5-mark question with no syllabus line.** Q4 is as
   reliable as Q1 and has no home in the current chapter list.
5. **Cloze without a word bank is a distinct, unlisted skill.** The syllabus
   mentions neither cloze nor word banks; the exam has done open cloze five
   years running.
6. **The 50–75 word budget with mandatory content points is a scored constraint,
   not a style note.** « Rédiger différents types de textes fonctionnels »
   understates a task where the word count and the checklist are both part of
   the 10 marks.
7. **Comprehension is signposted by paragraph.** The syllabus's
   « Justifier sa lecture par des relevés précis » is right in spirit, but the
   exam scaffolds it — questions are grouped under `Paragraphe n`, and in 2023
   cite line numbers. Practice that does not signpost is harder than the exam.
8. **What the syllabus promises and the exam never asks**: comparing the points
   of view of *several* texts (`g9fr-ce`), and producing a
   *compte-rendu* / summary (`g9fr-ee`). Neither appeared once in five papers.
