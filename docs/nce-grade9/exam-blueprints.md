# NCE Grade 9 — exam blueprints

One section per subject. A blueprint is only written here once at least one
paper has been read page by page; a subject with no section below has not been
analysed and **must not** have a paper generator built for it.

Read `source-inventory.md` first for what has actually been inspected.

---

## Mathematics (N510)

### Evidence base

Five papers. `NCE-2025-Mathematics.pdf` read in full (24/24 pages);
`2024-Mathematics.pdf` read at pages 1–10, 12, 14, 16, 18, 20; the 2021,
2021-2022 and 2023 covers read. Mark totals below come from the covers, which
state them explicitly.

### Cover and front matter — identical across all five years

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Awarding body | Mauritius Examinations Syndicate |
| Certificate | National Certificate of Education |
| Subject and code | **MATHEMATICS (N510)** |
| Duration | **2 hours** |
| Total marks | **100** |
| Answer medium | Candidates answer **on the question paper** |
| Additional materials | Geometrical instruments |
| Calculators | **NOT permitted** — stated on every year |
| Marks shown | In brackets `[ ]` at the end of each question or part question |
| Index number | Blank line at the top of the cover |

The cover always carries the same ten numbered instructions and a **For
Examiners' use** grid listing the odd page numbers plus Total and Signature.
2021 and 2021-2022 have four grid rows (Marker/Examiner · Team Leader · Quality
Controller · CE/ACE); 2023–2025 have three (Quality Controller dropped).

Body pages carry a `Marks` column heading top-right, a vertical rule down the
right margin, `©MES` bottom-left, the page number bottom-centre, and
`Please turn over` bottom-right on every page but the last. Odd pages carry a
small three-cell E / TL / CE box in the right margin. The paper ends with
**END OF PAPER**.

### Size across years

| Year | Questions | Body pages | Numbered | Marks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2021 (March/April) | 28 | 22 | — | 100 |
| 2021-2022 | 31 | 24 | 2–25 | 100 |
| 2023 | 31 | 20 | 2–21 | 100 |
| 2024 | 33 | 19 | 2–20 | 100 |
| 2025 | 31 | 23 | 2–24 | 100 |

**Derived blueprint: 31 questions (range 28–33), 100 marks, 2 hours.**

### ⚠ There are no named sections

Every year is a single unbroken run of numbered questions. There is no
"Section A"/"Section B", no optional questions, and no choice — the cover says
"Answer **ALL** questions". Any generator that prints section headers for
Mathematics is imitating PSAC, not NCE.

### Mark distribution

Mark-bearing parts counted from the layout text; 2021 extracts cleanly and sums
to exactly 100, which validates the method. Later years lose a few `[n]` tokens
to the same extraction damage described in `source-inventory.md`, so their
percentages are of what was recovered.

| Year | 1-mark | 2-mark | 3-mark | 4-mark | 5+ | Parts | Recovered |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2021 | 29 | 14 | 9 | 4 | — | 56 | 100/100 |
| 2021-2022 | 23 | 16 | 9 | 2 | — | 50 | 90/100 |
| 2023 | 27 | 15 | 6 | 3 | 1×[5] | 52 | 92/100 |
| 2024 | 25 | 15 | 6 | 2 | 1×[6], 1×[7] | 50 | 94/100 |
| 2025 | 22 | 19 | 7 | 3 | — | 51 | 93/100 |

**Derived blueprint per paper: ~52 mark-bearing parts, distributed
roughly 50% one-mark · 30% two-mark · 13% three-mark · 6% four-mark, with 0–2
extended parts of 5–7 marks at the very end.**

⚠ The 2024 `[6]` and `[7]` were checked on the page rather than assumed to be
extraction noise: Q31 (simultaneous equations from a shopping context, 6 marks)
and Q33 (quadratic from a square's area/perimeter, 7 marks). **The paper's
heaviest questions are its last ones**, and they are single-answer extended
problems, not multi-part.

### Progression — the shape a generated paper must reproduce

Measured on 2024 and 2025, which agree:

1. **Opening block of single 1-mark items.** 2025 Q1–Q10, 2024 Q1–Q11. One
   step each: column arithmetic, add/subtract like fractions, a cube root,
   order of a matrix, percent ⇄ fraction ⇄ decimal, a metric conversion, order
   of operations, expand a single bracket, factorise a common factor, one index
   law, collect like terms.
2. **One consolidated MCQ question.** 2025 Q11 with parts (a)–(g); 2024 Q12
   with parts (a)–(f). Always four options **A B C D**, always instructed
   "Circle the correct answer. Each item carries 1 mark." Options may be
   diagrams (2025 Q11(e): four sketch graphs) and the stem may carry a diagram
   (2025 Q11(c): an angle figure).
3. **Middle block, mostly two-part.** Marks 1–4 per part. This is where the
   diagram, table and graph stimuli sit.
4. **Closing block, heaviest.** 3–7 marks, multi-step, frequently a real-world
   context (finance, ratio, mixture, mensuration).

### Topics observed, with the year seen

Number: column arithmetic (24,25) · fractions (24,25) · decimals (24,25) ·

(25) · square/prime/composite number identification (24,25) · roots including

Algebra: simplify and collect (24) · expand (25) · factorise (24,25) · linear

(24,25) · inequalities and their number-line representation (24,25) ·

Geometry and mensuration: angles on a straight line and in parallel lines

Matrices: order of a matrix (25) · column vector arithmetic (25) · 2×2 matrix

### Stimulus and visual frequency

Counted on 2025 (full read) — 8 of 31 questions carry a visual, i.e. **≈26%**:
angle figure, four graph options, number line, Venn diagram, translation grid,
bar chart, labelled rectangle, 3-D tank, cylinder, coordinate grid, sector
figure. 2024 is comparable and adds a spinner, a data table and a blank chart
grid to be drawn on.

**Derived blueprint: about one question in four carries a diagram, grid, chart
or table, and at least one question per paper requires the candidate to DRAW on
a supplied grid or CONSTRUCT with compasses.**

### Answer-space conventions

- Objective answers get a dotted `Answer: .................` line, right-aligned,
  with the mark in brackets after it.
- The **unit is pre-printed after the line** when the question fixes one:
  `mL`, `g`, `m`, `cm`, `cm²`, `cm³`, `days`, `balls`, `students`,
  `goals per match`, `square units`, and `°` set above the line.
- A currency answer pre-prints `Rs` **before** the line.
- A named unknown pre-prints the label: `Answer: x = ...`, `Answer: A = ...`,
  `Answer: h = ...`, `Answer: angle a = ...`.
- Two or more unknowns get two or more blanks on one line:
  `x = ......, y = ......`; `x = ...... or x = ......`.
- An ordering answer gets one blank per item: `......, ......, ......, ......, ......`.
- Working space is generous and blank — a 3-mark item gets roughly a third of a
  page, a 6–7-mark item most of one.
- Some questions have **no answer line at all** because the response is made on
  the stimulus: circle a number in a list, tick one of several boxes, shade a
  Venn region, complete boxes on a number line, complete a table, draw a graph,
  construct a bisector.

⚠ **Six of these are not automatically markable and must not be replaced by an
easier MCQ.** They are a real part of the paper — at least three per year — and
belong in printable mode with a marking rubric.

### Numbering

Two levels are normal: `12.` then `(a)`, `(b)`. Three levels occur — 2025 Q31
runs `(a)(i)`, `(a)(ii)`, `(b)`; 2024 Q26 runs `(a)(i)`, `(a)(ii)`, `(b)(i)`,
`(b)(ii)`. A generator must support depth 3.

### Dependencies between parts

Real and explicit. 2025 Q17 is "(a) Find m … (b) **Hence**, solve …"; 2025 Q30
is "(c) **Using your answers to part (a) and part (b)**, find the perimeter";
2024 Q16 is "(b) Draw a number line to represent the inequality in **part (a)**".
A generated paper must be able to state that part B consumes part A's answer,
and a mark scheme must allow follow-through credit.

### Stimulus reuse

2025 Q28 and Q30 each share one stimulus across their parts (a tank then a
cylinder it is poured into; a sector figure used by three parts). The stimulus
is printed **once**, above the parts. This is the "shared stimulus with linked
subquestions" the assessment schema has to represent.

### Data given in the stem instead of assumed

Because calculators are banned, the paper **supplies** any value a candidate
could not compute: `[Given sin 60° = 0.87, cos 60° = 0.5, tan 60° = 1.73]`
(2025 Q30), `(Use π = 22/7)` (2025 Q30(b)), `[√5 = 2.236, √50 = 7.071]`
(2024 Q18). Generated questions must do the same, and the values must be
consistent with the answer the mark scheme expects.
