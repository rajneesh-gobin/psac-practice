# Product roadmap

This is a living roadmap for the parent and child experience. Update it as
priorities change; completed items should retain a short outcome note.

## Now

1. **Continue [content coverage plan](content-coverage-plan.md).**
   - Resume at Grade 4 French.
   - Ensure every Grade 4–6 syllabus subsection has at least 20 appropriate,
     varied questions, hints, and explanations.
   - Rebuild and audit every completed subject.

2. **Daily mission for children.**
   - A clear, small goal such as “Complete 5 questions today”.
   - Show progress, a gentle reward, and a clear next action.

3. **Today at a glance for parents.**
   - In plain language: study time, questions answered, accuracy, chapters
     practised, and one suggested next step for each child.

## Next

4. **Friendly parent onboarding.**
   - Three clear setup steps: add child, choose grade, choose a daily goal.
   - Explain Parent PIN, child PIN, shared-device switching, and separate
     parent accounts in simple language.

5. **One child-friendly “I’m stuck” journey.**
   - Hint first, then a worked example, then a suitable explanation/video.
   - Keep the existing help tools available without making the child hunt for
     them.

6. **Motivation and progress layer.**
   - Improve daily streaks, chapter-completion stars, badges, avatar rewards,
     and celebrations for effort and improvement.

## Later / premium candidates

7. **Personalised revision plans.**
   - Convert weaknesses and an exam date into short daily tasks.

8. **Parent reports and printable revision packs.**
   - Weekly progress summary, targeted worksheets, timed papers, answer sheets,
     and a shareable parent/teacher report.

9. **Premium learning support.**
   - Unlimited targeted practice, detailed explanations, curriculum-linked
     videos, multi-child family access, and advanced analytics.

## Product principles

- Children should see a simple mission, not a complicated school portal.
- Parents should understand their child’s progress within ten seconds.
- Keep safety and privacy clear on shared devices.
- Sell deeper support and personalisation, not basic access to learning.
