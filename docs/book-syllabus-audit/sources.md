## Reproducible records

- `book-inventory.json`: filenames, page counts, text sparsity indicators, contents-page references and SHA-256 hashes of all 33 source PDFs.
- `app-syllabus-snapshot.json`: the 25 evaluated local manifests captured for this comparison, including notes and subsections.
- `chapter-mapping.json`: all 235 mapping entries.
- Working text extracts and rendered checks are under `tmp/book-audit/`; these are intermediates, not replacement textbooks.
- Visual review covered one contents page per PDF plus 21 additional pages, including primary Maths learning objectives, Grade 7 angles/sequences, Grade 8 missing Maths chapters, English continuous-perfect tenses, French image reading/participle agreement, and science energy activities.
- Supplementary contents and selected body pages were also read as extracted text. No claim is made to have visually inspected all 4,663 pages or to have evaluated every exercise.
